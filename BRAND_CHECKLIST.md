# SarkariExamHub — Domain & Indian Trademark Search Checklist

This guide provides official, verifiable procedures for conducting a preliminary brand clearance for **SarkariExamHub** in India.

> [!NOTE]
> Availability of a domain or trademark name cannot be assumed or guaranteed without performing live queries on the official Indian Trademark Registry database and ICANN/NIXI registry databases.

## 1. Domain Name Availability Checks

When registering web domains for an Indian educational platform, the most important TLDs are:
- `sarkariexamhub.in` (India country code top-level domain — primary recommendation)
- `sarkariexamhub.com` (Global commercial top-level domain)
- `sarkariexamhub.co.in` (Indian commercial domain)
- `sarkariexamhub.org.in` (Non-commercial/educational organization in India)

### Official Domain Verification Steps
1. **Official .IN Registry (NIXI)**:
   - Visit the official National Internet eXchange of India registry: [registry.in](https://www.registry.in).
   - Enter `sarkariexamhub` in the WHOIS lookup box.
   - If the status is `AVAILABLE`, proceed to register it through any NIXI-accredited registrar.
2. **Global WHOIS (.COM / .NET)**:
   - Perform a WHOIS query through an ICANN-accredited registrar or [lookup.icann.org](https://lookup.icann.org).
3. **Tips for Registration**:
   - Enable **WHOIS Privacy Protection** to keep your administrative email and contact number private.
   - Register both `.in` and `.com` simultaneously if both are available to protect your brand identity.

## 2. Indian Trademark Search Procedure (IP India)

In India, trademarks are registered and administered by the **Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (CGPDTM)** under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

### Relevant Trademark Classes for SarkariExamHub

| Nice Class | Description | Scope Covered by SarkariExamHub |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Class 41** | **Education & Entertainment** | Education services, conducting examinations, mock test series, publication of study materials, coaching institutes, online educational courses. |
| **Class 42** | **Science & Technology / Software** | Software as a Service (SaaS), cloud-hosted educational platforms, AI question import software, website hosting. |
| **Class 38** (Optional) | Telecommunications | Portal communication, digital content transmission. |

---

### Step-by-Step Trademark Public Search

1. **Access the Official Portal**:
   - Go to the official Intellectual Property India Public Search portal:
     **[ipindiaonline.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch)**
2. **Select Search Type**:
   - You must conduct three separate searches:
     - **Wordmark Search**: Direct text matching.
     - **Phonetic Search**: Sound-alike matching.
     - **Well-Known Marks Search**: Checking against protected Indian brands.
3. **Running the Wordmark Search**:
   - **Search type**: `Wordmark`
   - **Wordmark**: `SARKARIEXAMHUB`
   - **Class**: `41` (then repeat with `42`)
   - Click **Search**.
   - If no identical results appear, test wildcard variations:
     - `SARKARI EXAM*`
     - `EXAMHUB*`
4. **Running the Phonetic Search**:
   - Switch search type to `Phonetic`.
   - Enter `SARKARIEXAM` or `SARKARI EXAM HUB`.
   - Enter Class `41`.
   - Examine conflicting or phonetically confusing marks (e.g., marks with similar pronunciations that could confuse consumers).
5. **Analyze the Search Results**:
   - For every listed trademark, inspect its **Status**:
     - `Registered`: Strong conflict if goods/services overlap.
     - `Pending` / `Objected` / `Opposed`: Potential future conflict.
     - `Abandoned` / `Refused` / `Removed`: Inactive, lower risk.

## 3. Important Statutory & Legal Considerations for "Sarkari"

Because the Hindi word *"Sarkari"* translates to *"Government"*, several Indian legal guidelines apply:

1. **Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950**:
   - You must **never** suggest government patronage, official government endorsement, or government department status unless officially sanctioned.
   - You cannot use the official State Emblem of India (Ashoka Lion Capital) or government department logos.
2. **Prominent Independence Disclaimer**:
   - SarkariExamHub already includes this mandatory disclaimer in its footer and About page:
     > *"SarkariExamHub is an independent educational platform for preparation purposes. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government body or recruitment board."*
3. **Distinctiveness**:
   - The word "Sarkari Exam" is descriptive of competitive exam preparation. Adding the suffix "Hub" creates a composite mark (`SarkariExamHub`).
   - For strongest trademark protection, file the logo/device mark (composite brand with distinct emblem and typography) rather than a bare descriptive phrase.

## 4. Next Steps for Brand Filing

1. **Preliminary Clearance**: Conduct the searches on `ipindiaonline.gov.in` as described above.
2. **Consult a Registered Trademark Attorney**: A licensed Indian Trademark Attorney can prepare a Formal Search Report and opinion.
3. **File Application Form TM-A**:
   - Filed online through the IP India e-filing portal.
   - Official government fee for Individual / Startup / Small Enterprise: **₹4,500** per class (as of standard e-filing rates).
   - Once filed, you can use the **TM** symbol.
   - Once registration is granted after publication in the Trademark Journal, you can use the registered **®** symbol.
